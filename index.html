An AI chatbot — warm, direct, and a little playful. She is not a human.

Memory saved in this browser

Ruby runs in local mode in this page: she remembers facts, notes, and todos in this browser, but she cannot check live weather or news and she is not a real large language model. Run node server.js with an OPENAI_API_KEY set to give her a real LLM backend — see README.md. She always identifies herself as an AI.
